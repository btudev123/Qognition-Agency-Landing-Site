# Anti-slop

Hard bans on every agent's written output. A client's `brand-voice.md` overrides this file where the two disagree.

## Banned phrases

Never write these, in any tense or variation:

- "in today's fast-paced world" (and every "in today's <adjective> <noun>" opener)
- "unlock" — as in unlock growth, unlock potential, unlock value
- "elevate" — as in elevate your brand
- "seamless" / "seamlessly"
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "delve"
- "landscape" as a metaphor — "the marketing landscape", "the competitive landscape"
- "leverage" as a verb — use "use"
- "it's not just X, it's Y" — and every variant of the not-just construction
- "navigate" as a metaphor for dealing with something
- "robust", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class", "world-class", "supercharge", "turbocharge"
- "empower", "harness", "unleash", "revolutionize", "transform" as filler verbs
- "at the end of the day", "when it comes to", "the fact of the matter is"

## Banned structures

- **Em-dash asides as a rhetorical tic.** One per section at most. If a sentence needs three, it needs to be two sentences.
- **Opening a paragraph by restating the question.** Start with the answer.
- **Closing with a summary of what was just said.** The reader read it. End on the next action or stop.
- **Rule-of-three lists** used for rhythm rather than because there are exactly three things.
- **Rhetorical questions as section openers.** "So what does this mean for your business?" — delete.
- **Hedging stacks.** "may potentially help to possibly improve" — say what it does.
- **Headers that promise and don't deliver.** "The Secret to X" followed by generic advice.

## Required

- **Specific nouns.** "Google Ads Performance Max" not "paid channels". "The pricing calculator" not "your conversion assets".
- **Real numbers, or none.** A number in output is a number from the client context, a connected data source, or a file the operator supplied. Otherwise `[NEEDS DATA: <what>]`. Never a plausible-sounding estimate.
- **Active voice.** "Marcus cut the budget" not "the budget was reduced".
- **Short sentences.** If a sentence runs past about 25 words, it is usually two.
- **One idea per paragraph.**
- **Claims carry evidence or a date.** "Ranks #3 for X (checked 2026-09-01)" or nothing.

## The test

Read the draft aloud. If any sentence sounds like it could appear on any competitor's website with the brand name swapped, it is slop. Rewrite it with something only this client could say.
