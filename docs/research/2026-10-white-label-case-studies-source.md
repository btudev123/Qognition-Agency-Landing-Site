## 02 / FALCON SECURITY: Enterprise B2B lead generation
USA-focused engagement. Estimated reporting window: 15 September 2025 to present.
Reported scope: company-page positioning, executive branding, named-account outreach, B2B lead generation and qualification handoff.
- Target market: large US enterprises in banking, healthcare, energy, manufacturing and technology. Validate service fit before including an account.
- Buying group: CISOs, CIOs, Heads of Cybersecurity and IT Security. Include SOC, IAM/PAM and GRC owners where the confirmed service supports their use case.
- Account-based execution: research named accounts and stakeholders; connect approved thought leadership to an account-specific issue; follow up with a discovery offer.
- Sales handoff: record need, role, account fit, timing and agreed next step. Pass qualified conversations to sales for explicit acceptance.
- Campaign performance from Qognition OS dashboard: MQLs 114 · Accepted SQLs 50 · Meetings booked 41 · Meetings held 33 · Opportunities 11.
- Calculation: 3,000 unique first touches x 3.8% = 114 MQLs. Subsequent conversions: 44% to accepted SQL, 82% to booked meeting, 80% to held meeting and 33% to opportunity, rounded at each step.
- Commercial estimate: 11 opportunities x US$60,000 illustrative scoped-project value = US$660,000.

## 03 / BRIXIO: AI search and enterprise demand
Reported duration: one year of SEO/content and six months of lead generation, both ongoing.
Reported scope: AI SEO, GEO and prompt research, whitepaper work and B2B lead generation. Lead funnel ran 3 March to 21 September 2026, within the year-long content engagement.
- UAE audience: Dubai and Abu Dhabi enterprise teams in banking, aviation, healthcare, energy, telecom and conglomerates; 500-10,000 employees is an assumed selection band.
- Buying group: CISO, CIO, Head of Cloud/Infrastructure, Network Security, Security Architecture and SOC, with procurement involved in evaluation.
- AI SEO / GEO: technical discovery fixes, service-page structure, SME content and fixed nonbranded prompt panels. Measure mentions, citations, tracked referrals and qualified conversions separately.
- Lead generation: named-account LinkedIn outreach using approved technical insights and discovery/audit offers, followed by qualification and sales handoff.
- Results: 92 estimated MQLs · 39 accepted SQLs · 32 booked / 26 held · 9 opportunities.
- Funnel inputs: 1,500 contacts x 3.6% = 54 outbound MQLs; 10,000 search sessions x 0.30% = 30; 400 tracked AI referrals x 2% = 8. Subsequent stage rates: 42%, 82%, 81% and 35%.
- Growth: monthly organic sessions rise from 1,200 to 2,400 (+100%) across the six-month model. Nine opportunities at US$48,000 first-year value imply US$432,000 pipeline.

## 04 / TALENTERA: Paid and organic B2B acquisition
Reported duration: three months, ongoing. Estimated window: 15 June to 21 September 2026.
Reported scope: LinkedIn advertising, B2B lead generation, PPC and AI SEO. US$20,000 is total LinkedIn media spend over the three months, excluding agency fees. No separate search-ad budget.
- Saudi ICP: employers with sustained recruitment needs in finance, healthcare, retail and enterprise services; assumed company size 200-5,000 employees. Riyadh, Jeddah and the Eastern Province. Target CHRO/HR Director, Head of Talent Acquisition, Recruitment Manager and HR Operations, with HRIS/IT and procurement as influencers.
- Paid: US$20,000 at US$12 CPC = 1,667 clicks; 6.5% conversion = 108 form submissions; 90% valid/unique = 97 leads; 60% = 58 paid MQLs; cost per MQL US$345.
- Results: 124 estimated MQLs · 52 accepted SQLs · 41 booked / 32 held · 10 opportunities.
- Other acquisition: 32 outbound MQLs from 900 contacts; 30 organic MQLs from 6,000 sessions; 4 MQLs from 200 tracked AI referrals. Stage rates: 42%, 79%, 79% and 30%.
- AI SEO: monthly organic sessions of 1,700, 2,000 and 2,300 imply +35.3% from first to last model month.
- Execution: ATS-intent content, role-specific ads, demo offers and employer-side qualification; candidates and jobseekers excluded.
- Commercial estimate: 10 opportunities x US$21,000 first-year value = US$210,000 pipeline.

## 05 / SOPHOS: Security operations demand
Reported duration: eight months, ongoing. Estimated window: 1 January to 21 September 2026.
Reported scope: USA-facing LinkedIn, B2B lead generation, AI SEO and GEO, focused on MDR/security-operations demand.
- Target accounts: US commercial organisations, assumed 250-5,000 employees. Healthcare, financial services, manufacturing and technology; validate regional buying authority.
- Decision-makers: CISOs, CIOs, Heads of Cybersecurity, SOC and IT Security. Qualify current tools, coverage gaps, endpoint scale and evaluation timing.
- LinkedIn / ABM: 600 candidate accounts with four contacts each: 2,400 unique first touches. Executive-risk, SOC-operations and IT-owner messaging.
- AI SEO / GEO: selected MDR-intent pages, cited technical explanations, comparison criteria and nonbranded prompt monitoring. Attribute only measured campaign-page activity.
- Sales coordination: validate acceptance and next steps; respect existing customer and channel ownership. Keep end-customer opportunities separate from MSP recruitment.
- Results: 111 estimated MQLs · 48 accepted SQLs · 39 booked / 32 held · 10 opportunities.
- Funnel inputs: 72 outbound MQLs from 2,400 contacts at 3%; 30 from 12,000 organic sessions at 0.25%; 9 from 600 tracked AI referrals at 1.5%. Stage rates: 43%, 81%, 82% and 31%.
- Growth example: organic sessions rise from 1,100 to 2,100 (+90.9%) across the eight-month model.
- Ten opportunities at US$72,000 first-year value imply US$720,000 pipeline.
