## 2. The definition

**Section heading**
## What SEO covers here

SEO is the Google search results page. Ten blue links, site links, the things that have been there
for twenty years. It is the surface people mean when they say "we rank well."

Separate from [Local SEO](/local-seo), which is Maps and the local pack. Separate from
[AI SEO](/ai-seo), which is Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. Separate from [GEO](/geo), which is
the assistants.

---

## 3. Why it still matters

**Section heading**
## Two reasons, and the second one is newer

**Reason one.** Buyers still search. A CFO comparing advisory firms still opens ten tabs. The blue
links are still where a considered B2B purchase gets researched in depth.

**Reason two.** AI Overviews are assembled from pages Google already holds and already trusts for the
topic. Assistants retrieve heavily from sources that perform in search. A site that does not rank is
not in the candidate set for either. Cancelling SEO to fund GEO is cancelling the input to fund the
output.

---

## 4. The work

- **Technical.** Crawlability, indexation, rendering, site speed, internal linking, canonical and
  redirect hygiene. Server-rendered HTML for anything you want extracted.
- **Architecture.** One page per buyer question, not one page per keyword variant. Pages that do not
  answer something unique get consolidated, not published.
- **On-page.** Answer-first structure, the question as the heading, the answer in the first two
  sentences, specifics your competitors cannot copy because they do not do the work you do.
- **Topical depth.** Coverage of a subject a buyer would recognise as complete, built out in a
  planned order rather than one post a month.
- **Authority.** Earned links and mentions from industry publications, associations and partners.
  No link buying, no private blog networks.
- **Pruning.** Pages with no impressions and no links get consolidated and redirected. A large page
  count is not an asset.
- **Reporting.** Rankings, Search Console performance, and the conversions your CRM recorded.

---

## 5. What we will not do

Publish city-by-service page combinations because the URL exists. Substitute an industry name into a
template and call it a page. Buy links. Chase a keyword count. Google's spam policy judges unoriginal
content that adds no value regardless of how it was made, and a page that only exists because a
combination is possible is exactly that.

## 6. FAQs

**How long does SEO take?**
Technical fixes show in weeks. Content and authority work compounds over months. We ask for three
months before you judge it, and we report monthly so you can see the direction before then.

**Can you guarantee a ranking?**
No. Google changes its results continuously and we do not control it.

**Do we still need SEO if we are investing in AI search?**
Yes, and the dependency runs that way round. AI answers are built from what already ranks.

**Can we buy SEO on its own?**
