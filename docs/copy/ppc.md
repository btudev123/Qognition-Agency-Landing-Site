## 2. Why this is a separate offer

**Section heading**
## Different economics, different decision

**Body**

The search visibility retainer is a compounding programme with a three-month horizon. Paid media is
a spend decision that can be turned on and off this afternoon, priced against the media budget rather
than against scope.

Bundling the two hides which one is working. It also creates a conflict, because an agency paid a
percentage of ad spend has an incentive to recommend more ad spend. Separating them keeps that
conversation honest and lets you stop one without stopping the other.

## 4. What matters in B2B paid media

**Track the sale, not the form fill.** A multi-week consideration cycle means the platform's
conversion count and your closed revenue are different numbers. Without offline conversion import
from your CRM, the bidding is optimising for the wrong thing.

**Capture click IDs.** `gclid`, `wbraid`, `gbraid`, `li_fat_id` and `msclkid` have to land on your
lead record at the moment of capture, or importing closed deals back to the platform later is
impossible.

**Attribution has to outlive the session.** A buyer who first clicks an ad in March and books in May
loses their first touch entirely if attribution is stored per session.

**Negative keywords are most of the job** in categories where job seekers, students and competitors
outnumber buyers.

## 5. Who it suits

Firms with an established sales process, a follow-up motion that works, and budget to run
consistently for at least three months. Paid search does not fix a pipeline that leaks after the
lead arrives.

Not a fit if you are testing whether anyone wants what you sell, or if nobody calls the leads back.
