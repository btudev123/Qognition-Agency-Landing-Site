## Delta 1. §4, the categories paragraph

Current:

> **Categories.** The primary category does more work than anything else on the Profile. A commercial
> insurance agency categorised as "Insurance agency" is competing with every personal lines
> storefront in the county. Pick the category that describes the business you want, not the business
> you have listed.

Replace with:

> **Categories.** The primary category does more work than anything else on the Profile. A commercial
> insurance agency listed as "Insurance agency" competes with every personal lines storefront in the
> county. A precision machine shop listed as "Manufacturer" competes with a furniture plant. An MSP
> listed as "Computer support and services" competes with the shop that fixes laptops. Pick the
> category that describes the business you want, not the business you were categorised as when
> somebody claimed the Profile in 2014.

Why. One example implied one audience. Three examples cover three Tier S verticals and each one is a
mistake a reader can check on their own Profile in under a minute, which is the point of the
paragraph.

## Delta 2. §4, the review velocity paragraph

Current opens:

> **Review velocity, which is the hard part.** B2B clients do not review their broker, their
> accountant or their MSP unprompted.

Replace the first two sentences with:

> **Review velocity, which is the hard part.** B2B clients do not review their broker, their
> accountant, their MSP or their contract manufacturer unprompted. Review counts in these categories
> are low, which means every review moves further than it would in a consumer category.

Then keep the existing sentence about asking at the right moment, and **extend its list of moments**
so it is not an insurance list:

> The fix is asking at the right moment: after a renewal, after a resolved claim, after a filing
> deadline is met, after an incident is contained, after a first article passes inspection, after a
> migration lands without downtime.

## Delta 3. §7, add two FAQs

The existing three stay. Add:

> **We are a manufacturer with one plant and national customers. Does any of this apply?**
> The map pack will not sell you a machining contract. The Profile still matters, because it is one
> of the most structured descriptions of your company that Google holds, and it is one of the sources
> an assistant checks when it decides whether it knows who you are. A Profile that names the wrong
> category, the wrong service list or an address that disagrees with your site is manufacturing
> ambiguity about your own identity. Fixing it is cheap and it is a prerequisite for the AI work,
> not an alternative to it.

> **Can we buy Local SEO on its own?**
> You can come to us wanting only local work. What you will be quoted is one retainer that includes
> all four search layers, because they share the same entity and technical foundation and splitting
> them would mean charging you twice for it. Paid media is the one thing quoted separately, priced
> against your media budget. [See pricing](/pricing) · [How paid works](/ppc)
