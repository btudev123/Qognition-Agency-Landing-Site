## 2. The definition

**Section heading**
## What GEO means here

**Body**

GEO, generative engine optimisation, is the work of getting a firm referenced and recommended inside
AI assistants: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Microsoft Copilot. An assistant answering
"who should I hire for this" assembles that answer from sources it retrieves about you, then names a
handful of firms. GEO is the work of being in that set.

It is not the same as AI SEO. AI SEO is Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, which run on Google's
index and report into Search Console. GEO covers systems Google does not operate, that retrieve
differently, that change without notice, and that publish no reporting whatsoever.

## 3. What GEO is not

**Section heading**
## The markup myth

**Body**

There is no tag you add to your website that makes an assistant recommend you. Schema helps a
machine understand what a page is. An `llms.txt` file offers crawlers a content index. Both are
worth doing, both take an afternoon, and neither is the mechanism.

The mechanism is that these systems assemble an answer from sources they retrieve, and most of those
sources are not your domain. In the largest published sample we have found, across 1,960 insurance
prompts producing 34,238 citations, insurer-owned domains accounted for **4.6%** of citations and
the single most-cited domain was Reddit at 3.5%
([Wellows, 12 August 2026](https://wellows.com/blog/ai-visibility-for-insurance-marketing-agencies/)).
That is one vertical, from a vendor with an interest in the finding, and it still points at the
thing that matters: your own website is a minority of the answer.

If an agency is selling you GEO as an on-page technique, they are selling you the 5%.

## 4. What moves this surface

**Section heading**
## Two things, in this order

### Entity certainty

A model that is not sure who you are will not recommend you. Your firm's name, location, what you do,
which states or markets you serve, and who works there have to agree across every source that
describes you: your website, your schema, your Business Profile, your directory and association
listings, your review profiles and your team's public profiles.

This sounds like housekeeping. It is the single most common reason a competent firm is absent from
assistant answers, and it is the cheapest thing on this page to fix.

### Third-party evidence

After that, it is about what other people's content says about you, in places these systems retrieve
from:

- Industry association directories and membership listings
- Trade publications and industry press
- Review platforms with real, specific reviews
- Comparison and alternatives content
- Commentary and interviews published under the names of your actual people
- Community threads where buyers ask each other who to use

Named people do a disproportionate amount of the work here. A system retrieving commentary on a
niche topic finds a person who wrote it. Firms without public experts are represented only by their
marketing copy, which is the least retrievable kind of content there is.

## 6. What we will not do

**Section heading**
## The line

Community content is heavily represented in what these systems cite. That creates an obvious
temptation, and the answer is no.

We do not post fake reviews. We do not create accounts to recommend a client in a forum. We do not

What we do instead is make your actual people visible in the places their buyers already read, under

## 7. Measurement

**Section heading**
## How this gets reported, honestly

**Body**

No assistant publishes an impressions report. There is no Search Console for ChatGPT. So GEO
measurement is built, not collected, and you should understand what it is before you buy it.

**The prompt set.** A fixed set of your buyers' questions, run on a schedule, across the platforms
in scope, from clean sessions in a stated location. We record the answer, the firms named and the
sources cited where the platform shows them. Month over month, the same set, so the comparison means
something.

**Referral traffic.** Visits arriving from `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai` and the other assistant
domains are visible in your analytics. Volumes are usually small. The intent is usually high, because
the visitor arrived already shortlisted.

**Conversions.** What your CRM recorded, tied back to source where the source survived the journey.

**The limits, stated plainly**

These systems are not deterministic. The same prompt can return different answers on different days.
Answers are personalised by account history and location. Models and retrieval change without
notice. A prompt set is a sample of a very large space of possible phrasings.

So a GEO report is a dated observation of a sample, not a ranking. We will never tell you your
position in ChatGPT, because there is no such thing.

## 9. FAQs

**Can you guarantee we get recommended by ChatGPT?**
No. We do not control it, it changes without notice, and anyone promising placement in a system they
do not own is guessing. We commit to the work, a prompt set you can inspect, and a dated record.

**How long does GEO take?**
The entity work lands in weeks. The third-party evidence layer is slower, because it depends on other
people publishing, and we ask for three months before judging it.

**Do we need to be on Reddit?**
Not by posting as yourselves in disguise, no. If your buyers discuss your category in communities,
that matters, and the honest routes in are your experts answering under their real names and being
worth quoting. We will tell you whether your category has that kind of community at snapshot stage.

**Our competitors get named and we do not. Why?**
Usually one of three things: they rank and you do not, their entity is unambiguous and yours
disagrees with itself across sources, or third parties write about them and not about you. The
snapshot tells you which of the three applies to you.

**Is GEO replacing SEO?**
No. Assistants retrieve from the web, and a lot of that retrieval favours sources that already
perform in search. Classic SEO became an input to a second surface rather than a standalone channel.
