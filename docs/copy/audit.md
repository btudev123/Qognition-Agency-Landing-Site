## 2. What it tests

**Section heading**
## What we run

### The prompt set

We write fifteen prompts from the questions your buyers actually ask, not from keywords. For a
commercial insurance agency that means renewal questions, class-of-business questions and
"who should I call" questions. For an MSP it means incident questions, compliance questions and
vendor comparison questions. You see the exact wording before we run it, and you can change it.

### The platforms

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Mode.
`[NEEDS DATA: confirm the final platform list and whether Claude is included in the free tier.]`

Each prompt runs in a clean session with no account history, from a stated location, on a recorded
date. We capture the answer, the firms named in it, and the sources cited where the platform shows
them.

### The site crawl

In parallel we crawl your site for the signals that feed those answers:

- Whether AI crawlers are allowed or blocked in `robots.txt`
- Whether a machine-readable content index exists at `/llms.txt`
- Sitemap health and whether your key pages are actually in it
- Organization and service schema, and whether your entity is described consistently
- Whether your core answers are in server-rendered HTML or only appear after JavaScript runs
- Conversion path: what a ready buyer is asked to do, and whether it is tracked

### The entity check

We compare how your firm is described across your website, your Google Business Profile, your major
directory listings and your team's public profiles. Disagreements here are one of the most common
reasons a model stays vague about a firm, and they are usually cheap to fix.

## 4. What it does not cover

**Section heading**
## What this is not

**Intro**
Written out because most free audits are not.

- **Not a full technical SEO audit.** We crawl a limited set of pages, not your whole site.
- **Not a backlink audit.** No link profile analysis, no toxic link review.
- **Not keyword research.** No volume data, no keyword list, no difficulty scores.
- **Not a competitive share-of-voice study.** We report who appeared alongside you in fifteen
  prompts. That is an observation, not a market share figure.
- **Not a forecast.** No traffic projection, no lead estimate, no revenue model. We do not have the
  data to build one and neither does anyone who offers you one for free.
- **Not an explanation of why a model chose its sources.** Retrieval is not observable from outside.
  We can tell you what was cited. We cannot tell you the mechanism that selected it.
- **Not paid media.** [PPC is a separate offer](/ppc).
- **Not compliance or legal review.** If you are in a regulated category, your own reviewer still
  has to sign off on anything published.

## 5. Why a sample is a sample

**Section heading**
## Fifteen prompts is a sample, and here is what that means

**Body**

Your buyers can phrase the same question hundreds of ways. Fifteen prompts cover the shapes we think
matter most for your business. A different fifteen could return a different picture, and we would
rather say that than let you read the result as a verdict.

Four things make any single AI observation unstable.

**The same prompt can return different answers.** These systems are not deterministic. Run a prompt
twice and the wording changes, and sometimes the firms named change with it.

**Answers are personalised.** Account history, saved memory, location and whether the user is signed
in all affect what comes back. We run from clean sessions in a stated location so results are
comparable to each other, but your buyer is not running a clean session.

**The systems change underneath you.** Models get updated, retrieval indexes refresh, and features
ship without announcement. An observation from six weeks ago describes six weeks ago.

**Absence in fifteen prompts is not absence.** Not appearing in our sample does not mean you never
appear. It means you did not appear in these prompts, on these platforms, on this date.

**Closing of section**
Which is why we never tell a firm it is invisible in AI search. We tell it what we saw, when we saw
it, and we run the same set again later so the comparison is worth something. That is the whole
method, and the snapshot is the smallest honest version of it.
