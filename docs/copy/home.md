## 3. The buying problem

**Section heading**
## Your buyer shortlists before they ever contact you

**Body**

A commercial buyer researching a vendor now asks an assistant first. They describe their situation
in a sentence, get three or four named firms back, and start their shortlist there. By the time
anyone contacts you, the comparison has already happened somewhere you cannot see.

That is a different problem from ranking. A search result gives the buyer ten options and lets them
choose. An assistant gives them three and explains why. If you are not one of the three, there is no
page two to be on.

The mechanics are not mysterious. An assistant assembles an answer from sources it retrieves, and
most of those sources are not your website. They are directories, association listings, review
platforms, trade press, forums and comparison pages. Your site tells the model who you are. Everyone
else's content decides whether you get named.

contacted. Gartner and 6sense both publish figures. No number goes on this page without a URL and a
date.]`

## 5. How the programme works

**Section heading**
## What the first 90 days look like

**Intro**
One retainer, one team, a fixed rhythm. Here is the sequence.

### Weeks 1 to 2: baseline

We record where you stand before we touch anything. A crawl of your site, your Search Console and
Business Profile data, your listing and review footprint, and a prompt set built from the questions
your buyers actually ask. We run that set across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Google AI
Mode and record what came back, with dates.

You get the prompt set as a document. You can add to it, remove from it, and argue with it. It is
the measurement instrument for the whole engagement, so it is worth arguing about.

### Weeks 3 to 6: the entity and the foundations

Assistants need to be certain who you are before they will recommend you. That means one consistent
description of your firm across your site, your schema, your Business Profile, your listings, your
association entries and your team's public profiles. Where those disagree, we fix the disagreement.

In parallel: technical SEO, site structure, and the pages that answer the specific questions your
buyers ask. This is the unglamorous half, and skipping it is why most GEO work produces nothing.

### Weeks 7 to 12: the evidence layer

Getting named in an AI answer usually depends on sources you do not own. So the work moves off your
site: association profiles, industry directories, review platforms, trade publications, expert
commentary under the names of your actual people, and comparison content that a model can retrieve
and attribute.

Named people matter more here than the firm does. A model retrieving commentary on contractor
liability will find a person who wrote it, not a logo.

### Ongoing: rerun, compare, report

The prompt set runs again on a fixed schedule. You see the same prompts, the same platforms, a new
date, and what changed. Alongside it: Search Console AI impressions, classic rankings, Maps
performance, and the conversions your CRM recorded.

**Honest note in the section**
We do not control ChatGPT, Google or Perplexity, and we will not tell you when you will appear in
them. What we commit to is the work, the measurement and the dated record of both.

## 9. FAQs

**Can you guarantee we appear in ChatGPT?**
No, and no one can. We do not own or control these systems, their retrieval changes without notice,
and the same prompt can return different answers on different days. What we commit to is the work,
a measurement instrument you can inspect, and a dated record of what changed.

**How is this different from the SEO we already pay for?**
Classic SEO targets Google's blue links. That work still matters, and it is inside our retainer,
because assistants retrieve from sources that rank. What is new is the entity work and the
off-site evidence layer that decides whether a model names you when it assembles an answer. Most SEO
retainers do none of that, and most do not measure any AI surface at all.

**Can you show me results from a firm like mine?**
Not yet, and we will not manufacture one. This category is young and the published case studies in
it are almost entirely B2B SaaS. What we can show you is the method, the measurement, and a snapshot
of your own situation before you pay anything. If proof of prior results in your exact vertical is
your buying requirement, we are not your vendor yet, and we will tell you that on the call.

**How long before anything moves?**
The technical and entity work shows up in weeks. The off-site evidence layer, which is the part that
drives assistant citations, is slower, and we ask for three months before you judge it. Anyone
quoting you a date for an AI citation is making it up.

**Do we have to write the content?**
No, we write it. We do need access to your people for roughly an hour a month, because the material
that gets retrieved and cited is specific, and specific means it came from someone who does the
work. Generic content written without you will not be retrieved.

**What do you need from us to start?**
Access to Google Search Console, Google Analytics, your Google Business Profile, and the ability to
publish changes to your website. Plus one person who can make decisions and one hour a month from
your subject-matter people.
