## 2. The definition

**Section heading**
## What AI SEO means here

**Body**

AI SEO is the work of getting a site referenced inside Google's AI-generated answers: AI Overviews,
which appear above the classic results, and AI Mode, which replaces the results page with a
conversational answer. Both are produced by Google, drawn from Google's index, and reported inside
Google Search Console.

It is a separate surface from GEO, which covers assistants Google does not operate: ChatGPT,
Perplexity, Claude and Copilot. Those retrieve from different places, respond to different work, and
publish no reporting at all. Most agencies use the two terms interchangeably. They are not
interchangeable, and treating them as one thing is how budgets get spent on the wrong half.

## 3. What moves this surface

**Section heading**
## What changes whether Google uses your page

### Being in the index and ranking well

AI Overviews are assembled from pages Google already holds and already trusts for the topic. Classic
search performance is therefore an input, not an alternative. A site that does not rank is not in the
candidate set. This is why AI SEO and SEO sit in the same retainer.

### Passage-level clarity

Google extracts passages, not pages. A page that states the answer plainly in the first two
sentences under a heading that matches the question is extractable. A page that opens with three
paragraphs of scene-setting is not. Most B2B service content fails on exactly this.

### Entity certainty

Google has to be confident who you are before it names you as a provider. That means a consistent
description of your firm across your site, your schema, your Business Profile and your listings.
Disagreement between those sources produces vagueness, and vague entities do not get named.

### Rendering

If the answer only appears after JavaScript runs, treat it as at risk. Server-rendered HTML for
anything you want extracted.

### Structured data

Organization, Service, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList markup, accurate and matching the visible page.
Schema does not force an AI Overview. It removes ambiguity about what the page is, which is a
different and more modest claim than the one most of this industry makes.

## 5. Measurement

**Section heading**
## The one AI surface with real reporting

**Body**

Search Console added generative AI performance reporting and rolled it out to all sites worldwide on
31 August 2026. It shows impressions from AI Overviews, AI Mode and generative AI features in
Discover, broken down by page, country, device and date.
[Google Search Central announcement](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports)

**The limit, stated plainly**

The report gives impressions. It does not give clicks. So we can show you that Google is surfacing
your pages in AI answers more often than it was, and we cannot tell you how many people clicked
through from those answers. Anyone showing you AI Overview click data is showing you an estimate.

**What we pair it with**

Classic Search Console performance, Google Business Profile insights, and the conversions your CRM
recorded. Impressions are an input. Booked calls are the measure.

## 7. FAQs

**Will AI Overviews cut our traffic?**
For informational queries, often yes, because the answer is given on the results page. For
vendor-selection queries the value moves from the click to the mention. Being named in the answer
that forms the shortlist is worth more than a visit from someone comparing definitions. We report
both so you can see the trade rather than take our word for it.

**Can you guarantee we appear in an AI Overview?**
No. Google decides when to generate one and what to cite, and it changes that behaviour without
notice. We do the work that puts you in the candidate set and we report what happened.

**Is schema markup the trick?**
No. Schema removes ambiguity about what a page is. It does not cause Google to cite you. Any agency
selling markup as the mechanism is selling you the cheapest part of the job.

**How is this different from the SEO we already buy?**
Ask your current provider for the generative AI performance report from Search Console. If they do
not know it exists, they are not working this surface.
