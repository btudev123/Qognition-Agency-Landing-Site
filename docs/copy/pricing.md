## 2. Why there is no number on this page

**Section heading**
## What we are not doing

**Body**

A published price is only honest when the thing being sold is identical for everyone. A single-office
CPA firm in one state and a fourteen-office insurance agency in six states are not buying the same
programme, and a "from" price would tell the second firm nothing useful.

So we scope first. The free snapshot or the walkthrough gives us what we need: your offices, your
markets, the questions your buyers ask, and the state of what you have now. The proposal after that
has a fixed monthly figure and a defined scope, not a range.

We are not hiding the price. Ask for it and you will have it after one conversation.

## 5. What drives the scope

**Section heading**
## Five things move the number

**Offices.** Each physical location is a separate local entity with its own Business Profile,
listings, reviews and reconciliation work. This is the largest single driver.

**Markets and lines.** A firm selling one thing to one kind of buyer needs one prompt set. A firm
selling four distinct lines to four kinds of buyer needs four, and four sets of content behind them.

**Prompts tracked.** The measurement instrument has a cost. More prompts across more platforms, run
more often, gives a more stable picture and takes more time to run and interpret.

**Assets produced.** Pages, expert content, comparison content and listing work. This is the part
that does the actual moving, and it is the part clients most often want to increase after month
three.

**Where you are starting from.** A site with no schema, inconsistent listings and no Business Profile
management needs foundation work that a well-maintained site does not. This shows up in the first
three months and then flattens.

## 7. FAQs

**Why can I not see a price?**
Because the scope varies enough that a published number would misinform most readers. One
conversation and you have a real figure against a real scope.

**Can we start with just GEO?**
No. GEO depends on the entity work and the search foundations underneath it. Selling it alone would
be selling you something that does not work.

**What happens after three months?**
You get a review of the prompt set rerun against the baseline, the Search Console AI data, and the
conversions your CRM recorded. Then you decide whether to continue. Month to month from that point.

**Is there a cheaper way to start?**
Yes, and we will raise it on the call if the retainer is not the right shape for you. There is a
one-off paid audit that goes considerably deeper than the free snapshot. We do not sell it from this
page because it only makes sense once we have both seen the snapshot.

**Do you work with our competitors?**
Not in the same category in the same market. We agree the exclusion in writing before we start.
