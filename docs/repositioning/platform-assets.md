# AI-platform presentation

Names and logos identify discovery surfaces; they are not customer logos, certifications or platform endorsements. Monitoring is scoped per engagement.

The existing OpenAI, Google, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Meta assets are reused from this repository. Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi and Baidu SVG icons were retrieved from Simple Icons on 2026-09-24 (https://simpleicons.org/; project https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). Underlying brand rights and guidelines continue to apply. Microsoft Copilot and Grok use neutral text monograms until suitable brand assets are available; no fabricated logo is used.

Source endpoints: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/mistralai, https://cdn.simpleicons.org/deepseek, https://cdn.simpleicons.org/qwen, https://cdn.simpleicons.org/kimi, https://cdn.simpleicons.org/baidu.
