# Qognition growth-site coverage and data lineage

Snapshot: 2026-09-24. Generated by `node scripts/report-growth-coverage.cjs`.

## Publication contract

- Registry coverage is distinct from public pages and index eligibility.
- `draft` and `review` marketing records do not resolve as public pages. Unknown combinations return 404.
- Navigation and indexing are independent flags on a published record.
- Indexable publication requires editorial context and a reviewed date. Sitemap dates use actual content dates.
- Deep subniche briefs are published for human browsing but currently noindex. They contain a distinct buyer focus and evidence checklist; they need deeper original examples before broad search expansion.
- Geographic record profiles are server-rendered, noindex reference utilities. They are not generated local agency landing pages or claims of a local office.
- Source-derived geographic history is retained. Historical, abandoned and destroyed populated-place records are excluded by default from catalog results.

## Explicit limits and follow-through

- “All niches” means the union of the supplied briefs plus existing retained service coverage, not every possible B2B category in the world.
- China and India export plays are geographic/business-model overlays, not duplicate industries. Headquarters, customer market, language and service footprint remain separate.
- Every combination is representable in the entity model; only explicitly authored/reviewed marketing combinations resolve. A complete Cartesian product is intentionally not published.
- Region and city records have standalone noindex reference profiles. Dedicated indexable city/industry/service pages require an original buyer and market brief; there is no blanket promise of local search demand.
- Sanity includes growth taxonomy, source and publication schemas. The checked-in manifest remains the application publication source for this release; changes in Sanity do not automatically override the code-reviewed manifest. A controlled editorial export/review is required for publishing changes.
- The 11-language routing/translation registry is implemented separately. Only complete published translations receive language alternates; the English catalog is not silently advertised as translated.
- No proof, office, certification, guaranteed citation or attributable revenue outcome is inferred from a taxonomy entry.
- Live search-demand data, client-approved vertical proof and independently sourced market benchmarks were not supplied for every niche or place. Those remain editorial inputs for later publication waves.
