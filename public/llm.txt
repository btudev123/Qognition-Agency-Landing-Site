## Entity Summary

Qognition Agency is an AI-native operating partner for founders and growth-stage companies. We run marketing, tech, finance, and automation so founders can build the business, not manage vendors. One SLA. One relationship. One standard of execution. Founded 2019. Offices in New York (NYC), London (LDN), Dubai (DXB), Bangalore (BLR), and Sydney (SYD).
